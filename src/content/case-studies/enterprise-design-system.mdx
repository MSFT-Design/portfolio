## Context

Led the definition of a unified design system across twelve product teams operating independently.

This work reflects a core specialization in building systems that scale across organizations.

## Problem

Fragmentation across teams resulted in:
- Duplicate components
- Inconsistent patterns
- Slower product delivery

## Approach

Conducted a full system audit to identify redundancy and misalignment.

Focused on creating a system that teams could adopt incrementally without disruption.

## System Design

Defined a layered architecture:

**Foundation Tokens**  
Base values for spacing, color, and typography

**Semantic Tokens**  
Mapped intent to visual output

**Component Tokens**  
Aligned with engineering implementation

Introduced a contribution model that allowed teams to extend the system while maintaining consistency.

## Outcome

- Reduced design to development time
- Improved consistency across products
- Enabled scalable collaboration between teams

## Reflection

Design systems require governance to scale effectively.

A shared system must support contribution without introducing fragmentation.

Strong foundations prevent long-term inefficiencies.
